## Description
Where was this photo taken — and in which year?

Every day, Mazi Türkiye shows you 5 real photographs from Türkiye, from 1880s Istanbul to today's Anatolian towns. Drop a pin on the map, pick the year, and see how close you were.

• 5 photos a day, same for everyone, new round at 04:00 (Türkiye time)
• Up to 1,000 points per photo: 500 for location, 500 for year
• After each guess: the right place on the map, the photo's short story and its source
• Challenge a friend with a link — same 5 photos, compare scores
• Live 1v1 duels (an AI opponent may be assigned if no player is found)
• Daily leaderboards and stats by era and region
• No ads, no account needed

Pro (one-time purchase, no subscription): hints, unlimited 1v1, archive of past days, full stats, streak protection.

All photos are public domain or openly licensed for commercial use, with author and license shown in the app. No AI-generated images.
