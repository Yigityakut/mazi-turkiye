# App Review notu (App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes)

Kopyalanacak metin (İngilizce; inceleme ekibi için):

```
Thank you for reviewing Mazi Türkiye.

• No login required: the daily game works with an anonymous session. Tap "Oyna" (Play) on the home screen.
  Sign in with Apple / Google is optional (Settings → Hesap) and only syncs scores across devices.
• Gameplay: each day 5 real historical/contemporary photos of Türkiye. Drop a pin on the built-in map
  and choose the year with the slider, then "Tahmin et" (Guess).
• Live 1v1 (home → "Canlı 1v1"): we first try to match a real player. If no player is found within
  ~2 minutes, an AI opponent is assigned. This is disclosed in our Terms (section 2) and on the
  matchmaking screen ("Rakip bulunamazsa yapay zekâ rakip atanabilir."). The AI opponent's name is
  taken from a fixed list of nicknames; we never claim fake player counts.
• In-app purchase: one non-consumable product "pro_lifetime" (Pro, one-time). To test, use a sandbox
  account: Home → Arşiv · Pro, or tap a hint chip ("Dönem ipucu") during a round → "Pro'yu aç".
  "Satın alımı geri yükle" (Restore) is on the same screen.
• Account deletion: Settings (gear icon) → "Hesabı sil" → confirm. Deletes the account and all data.
• Photos are public domain or CC0/CC BY/CC BY-SA licensed (Wikimedia Commons, Library of Congress
  Abdul Hamid II Collection). Author, source and license are shown after each guess and in
  Settings → Kaynaklar ve lisanslar. No AI-generated imagery.
• Notifications: we ask permission only after the user finishes their first daily round.
• The app is in Turkish. Key words: Oyna = Play, Tahmin et = Guess, Ayarlar = Settings.
```
