# Caption patterns, story templates, X drafts and community voice

> For tone of voice, bios and the attribution pattern see `profiles.md`; for what to post on which day see `../content-calendar.md`.
> Every `‹…›` gets filled in. Places marked **‹gerçek veri›** (real data) come only from the database or PostHog; no made-up numbers, no estimated numbers.

## 0. In every caption

- **The first line is the hook.** It is the only thing visible in the feed, so it must be a short, curiosity-sparking sentence.
- **One CTA.** By period:
  - 11–23 November: `24 Kasım'da App Store'da. Haber almak için link profilde.`
  - 24 November – Android: `Mazi Türkiye App Store'da, Android Aralık başında. Link profilde.`
  - After Android: `Mazi Türkiye App Store ve Google Play'de. Link profilde.`
- **Always an attribution line:** `Fotoğraf: ‹Eser sahibi›, ‹Kaynak›, ‹Lisans›`
  - Public domain → `kamu malı`; CC licences with their version (`CC BY-SA 4.0`).
  - If the author is unknown: `Fotoğraf: Bilinmiyor, Wikimedia Commons, kamu malı`.
- **Today's photos and answers are never written.**
- **A city hashtag gives the answer away.** No city or region hashtags on guess-it ("bil bakalım") posts (§8).
- **At most 2–3 emoji.** Hashtags go at the very end.
- **If there is an AI voice-over,** turn on the platform's "AI-generated" label and also write `Seslendirme: yapay zekâ` in the caption.

## 2. The photo's story (30–45 s video or 5-slide carousel)

**Rule:** The story uses only information written in the source: the Commons description, the LOC record, `photos.story_tr`. No "legend has it" narration, guessing or embellishment. If the year is not exact (`year_uncertainty = 1`), write "1890'lar civarı" (around the 1890s).

**Carousel slides:**

1. The photo and "Nerede, hangi yıl? Kaydırmadan önce tahmin et."
2. Location on the map and the year in large type.
3. Story: 2–3 short sentences from the source.
4. (Optional) "Bugün orası" (the place today): only with a current photo you took yourself or one that is openly licensed. No AI.
5. Source and attribution, with "Her gün 5 fotoğraf: Mazi Türkiye" below.

**Caption:**

> English summary: "Guess before swiping: where and when? Answer on slide 2", 2–3 sentences from the source, "Had you seen this photo before?", attribution, hashtags (city hashtags allowed here).

```
Kaydırmadan tahmin et: bu fotoğraf nerede, hangi yıl? Cevap 2. karede.

‹Kaynaktan 2–3 cümle. Ör. "Fotoğrafın kaynaktaki başlığı: ‹başlık›. ‹Kaynakta yazan bir ayrıntı›."›

Bu fotoğrafı daha önce görmüş müydün? 👇

Fotoğraf: ‹Eser sahibi›, ‹Kaynak›, ‹Lisans›
‹hashtag seti: burada şehir hashtag'i serbest›
```

## 3. Community stat (8 s card or single image)

**Rules:**

- **Past days only.** No stats about today's photo, because they hint at the answer.
- **If n < 100, no percentages.** Small samples mislead. Post "yesterday's hardest photo" instead.
- Only `mode = 'daily'` plays count; admin accounts are excluded. Percentages are rounded to whole numbers.
- **Note down the query and its result before posting,** so the source can be shown if anyone asks.

**Query (Supabase SQL):**

```sql
-- Dünün ‹N›. fotoğrafı (admin hariç, sadece günlük mod)
select count(*)                                               as n,
       round(100.0 * avg((abs(p.year_diff) <= 5)::int))      as yuzde_5_yil,
       round(100.0 * avg((p.distance_km <= 100)::int))       as yuzde_100_km,
       round(avg(p.score_location + p.score_year))           as ortalama_puan,
       round(avg(p.guess_year))                              as ortalama_yil_tahmini
from public.plays p
join public.profiles pr on pr.id = p.user_id
where p.day = public.game_day() - 1 and p.slot = ‹N› and p.mode = 'daily' and not pr.is_admin;
```

**Patterns:**

> English summary: four stat templates: % within 5 years; yesterday's average score and hardest photo; "wisdom of the crowd" average year guess vs the true year; % of pins within 100 km.

```
Dünkü ‹N›. fotoğrafı oyuncuların %‹gerçek veri›'i 5 yıl içinde bildi. Sen olsan?
```

```
Dünün ortalama skoru ‹gerçek veri› / 5.000. En çok zorlayan ‹N›. fotoğraftı: ortalama ‹gerçek veri› puan.
```

```
Kalabalığın bilgeliği: dünkü ‹N›. fotoğraf için yıl tahminlerinin ortalaması ‹gerçek veri›, doğru cevap ‹yıl›. ‹Tuttu! / Bu sefer tutmadı.›
```

```
Dün ‹N›. fotoğrafta oyuncuların %‹gerçek veri›'i iğnesini 100 km içine bıraktı. Haritada en çok şaşırtan fotoğraf buydu.
```

**Last line of the caption:** `Sayılar ‹tarih› günü oynanan turlardan, uygulama içi anonim verilerden.` plus the attribution line.

> English summary: "Today's theme: the day's 5 photos are ‹theme›, in the app until ‹end date›. The photo in this video isn't from the game set, so nothing is spoiled; still comment your guess."

## 9. Comment reply guide

1. **Speed:** Reply to every comment in the first hour. After that, two rounds a day, midday and evening.
2. **Celebrate the guess, never mock a wrong one.** Give proximity info: "100 km kadar doğudaydı" (about 100 km east), "8 yıl erken" (8 years early).
3. **If someone posts today's answers** (a spoiler for the in-game day), hide the comment and explain politely. On bonus or past-day videos, early answers are fine.
4. **Fact correction:**
   - Thank them and check the source.
   - If the comment is right, add a `Düzeltme: …` line to the caption and update the record in the app.
   - If not, show the source; don't argue.
5. **Politics, ethnic or religious arguments, provocation:** Don't engage; bring the topic back to the photo. Hide or delete hate speech and insults; block the account if needed.
6. **"Is this AI?" question:** A clear answer and the source link.
7. **Copyright notice:** Thank them, direct them to destek@maziturkiye.com, and remove the photo until the review is done (the process on the support page).
8. **Bug or complaint:** Apologise, give the support email, follow up. Never say "it works for me".
9. **Android question** (before 3 December): "Aralık başında geliyor; profildeki linkten e-postanı bırakırsan çıktığı gün haber veririz."
10. **Never:**
    - Reply from a second account.
    - Bought comments.
    - Ask for "give us 5 stars", or ask for reviews or ratings in exchange for a reward.
    - Badmouth another game.
11. **Tone:** Informal "sen", warm and short, at most one emoji.
